**Gadgets**

*What are wearable health devices?*

Digital health trackers, commonly referred to as “wearables”, help patients track various elements of their overall health, such as steps taken in a day or week, blood pressure, glucose, heart rate, and sleep analysis. This technology gives patients the ability to collect their own health data and can be transmitted to smartphones or computers for analysis and feedback.

*Glossary:*

1.	Wearable health devices – носимые устройства для мониторинга здоровья
2.	Digital health trackers – цифровые трекеры здоровья
3.	Wearables – носимые устройства (гаджеты)
4.	Steps taken – количество пройденных шагов
5.	Heart rate – частота сердечных сокращений (пульс)
6.	Sleep analysis – анализ сна

*Examples of wearable health devices*

1.	Blood pressure monitors: These wearables measure blood pressure levels throughout the day, providing long-term tracking and data for managing health conditions like hypertension.
2.	Glucose monitors: Wearable glucose monitors keep track of blood sugar levels in interstitial fluid in real-time, allowing patients with diabetes to manage their condition effectively. Some Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGMs) can connect with insulin pumps to automatically administer insulin during a glucose spike. Many CGMs today also have a corresponding smartphone app for viewing trends in glucose levels.
3.	Pain management devices: Using technology like transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation to provide pain relief and control muscle spasms, wearable pain management devices help patients with chronic pain so they can get back to their daily activities.
4.	Holter monitors: Portable ECGs (electrocardiograms) like Holter monitors help record the heart's electrical activity, detect irregularities, and can provide insights into overall heart health, including monitoring for atrial fibrillation and atrial flutter. Some ECG monitors can make it easier for cardiologists to diagnose common arrythmias depending on the device’s sensitivity.4
5.	Pacemakers: A small wearable device that’s implanted under the skin, pacemakers deliver electrical impulses that help maintain a normal heart rate and rhythm.
6.	Fitness Trackers: Fitness trackers are wearable devices that monitor physical activity and have risen in popularity for health-conscious consumers. Although the ways in which they integrate with HIT are still evolving, they can track valuable information such as steps taken, distance traveled, calories burned, and sleep patterns. They also track heart rate and sometimes include a GPS for location tracking.
7.	Smart Watches and Rings: Similar to fitness trackers, biosensors in smart watches and rings enable patients to track various health metrics like body temperature, oxygen and hydration levels, heart rate, activity and movement, and sleep analysis. Some models also include ECG capabilities as well.
